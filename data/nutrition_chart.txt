Institute of Nutrition - Daily Nutrition Requirements & Calorie Chart

SECTION 1: RECOMMENDED DAILY NUTRITIONAL INTAKE
According to the Institute of Nutrition, the daily recommended nutrient intake for average healthy adults is:
- Daily Caloric Intake: 2,000 to 2,500 kcal/day (varies by age, gender, and physical activity level).
- Protein: 50g to 70g per day (10-15% of daily calories).
- Carbohydrates: 225g to 325g per day (45-65% of daily calories).
- Total Fats: 44g to 78g per day (20-35% of daily calories).
- Saturated Fats: Less than 20g per day (<10% of total calories).
- Cholesterol: Less than 300 mg per day.
- Dietary Fiber: 25g to 38g per day (essential for digestive health and satiety).
- Sodium: Less than 2,300 mg per day.

Note: Actual daily intake for average adults often exceeds these recommendations, leading to excess caloric, protein, and fat consumption which can contribute to weight gain and obesity.

SECTION 3: DIETARY STRATEGIES FOR BMI & WEIGHT MANAGEMENT
- Caloric Deficit for Weight Loss: A daily deficit of 500 kcal relative to TDEE results in ~0.5 kg (1 lb) weight loss per week.
- High Protein Benefit: Consuming 20-30g of protein per meal increases satiety, preserves lean muscle mass during weight loss, and elevates thermogenesis.
- Fiber & Glycemic Control: High-fiber foods (>5g per serving) slow digestion, stabilize blood sugar levels, and reduce cravings.
- Hydration Guideline: Drinking 2.5 to 3 liters of water per day aids metabolic rate and helps distinguish thirst from hunger signals.
